Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Automate By Omnia provides a way to request deletion of personal data associated with our services or Meta-connected automations.

How to request deletion

Email hello@automatebyomnia.com with the subject Data Deletion Request. Include the email address or phone number connected to the service so we can identify your data.

What happens next

We will confirm receipt and delete or anonymize applicable personal data within 30 days, unless we must retain limited information for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.

Meta-connected services

You may also remove the app from your Facebook or Instagram settings to revoke future access. Email us to request deletion of data already processed or stored by our service.

Contact

For questions, email hello@automatebyomnia.com.
